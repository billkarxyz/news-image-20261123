# The World Today — Monday, 23 November 2026

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[news-image-20261123](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261123):
sum up the world's news of Monday 23 November 2026 in one image.

`entry.png` (1920×1280) is a newspaper front page, generated for this entry,
covering the day's biggest stories:

- **COP31 collapses** — the climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, ends with no
  deal; the EU and about 130 nations rejected a draft text that omitted fossil
  fuels. First COP to fail since Copenhagen in 2009.
- **Markets tumble** — Nikkei −5.06%, S&P 500 −3.7% as AI-bubble fears spread
  across Asia; gold rose as investors sought safety.
- **10 killed in Ukraine strike** — a Russian strike on an apartment block in
  Dymer, north of Kyiv; a 7-year-old boy was pulled alive from the rubble
  after 14 hours.
- **Democrats win big** — 258 House seats, the largest Democratic majority
  since 1992.
- **Liverpool 4–0 Man Utd** — Mauricio Pochettino's debut as United manager
  turns nightmare at Anfield; Leny Yoro sent off after 8 minutes.
- Also in the ticker: the Russian warship Merkury sunk off Libya, tankers
  ablaze near Hormuz, and the Riemann Hypothesis proof claim passing computer
  checks.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` (creating `/output` if needed).
Node 22+, standard library only, no dependencies.
